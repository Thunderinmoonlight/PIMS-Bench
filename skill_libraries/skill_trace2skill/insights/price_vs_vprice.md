# Lesson: PRICE vs VPRICE in SELL Table

Observed: 37% of tasks have ColR < 0.8, most commonly due to VPRICE confusion.
- VPRICE = volume-weighted average price (COMPUTED OUTPUT, not input)
- PRICE = unit selling price (what scenarios ask you to change)

Rule: When scenario says "raise price to $X", always use PRICE column, never VPRICE.
