
## Equipment Parameter Sheets

### K/D Swing Parameters (KD1, CD1)
- **Sheet**: KDSPARAM or UNITPARAM
- **KD1**: K factor for diesel in K/D swing equation
- **CD1**: C factor for diesel in K/D swing equation
- **Modification**: Change KD1 and CD1 values to adjust diesel yield from the K/D swing unit

### Coker Parameters
- **Sheet**: COKER or UNITCAPS
- **MAX**: Maximum capacity of the coker unit (in BPD or tons/day)
- **Modification**: Change MAX value to adjust coker throughput limit

### Diesel Price
- **Sheet**: PRICES or ECONPARAM
- **DSL PRICE**: Diesel product price (in $/bbl or $/ton)
- **Modification**: Change PRICE value for DSL to update diesel economics
